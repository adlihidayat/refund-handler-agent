# Customer Shipping Address Update Policy

1. **Eligibility Status**: Address change is allowed ONLY if the order status is "Processing" or "Pending".
2. **Ineligible Status**: Orders with status "Shipped" or "Delivered" CANNOT have their shipping address modified.
3. **Verification**: Always verify customer email and order ID before updating the shipping address.
4. **Audit Requirement**: The new address must be recorded accurately in the shop back office.
